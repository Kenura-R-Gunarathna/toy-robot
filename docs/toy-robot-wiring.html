Placement

  • Mount the two mics 40–65 mm apart, centre to centre, with their sound holes facing the same way. Measure the real spacing and set Mic spacing in menuconfig, because the direction estimate depends on it.
  • The angle reads 90° when the sound is straight ahead of the mic pair, and 0° or 180° toward either end. Two mics can't tell front from back.
  • Keep the speaker as far from the mics as you can, and point it away from them.
  • Keep the I2S wires short (under ~15 cm), especially SCK and BCLK.

Before powering on

  • Check that both mic VDD pins go to 3V3, not 5V.
  • Check mic A L/R → GND and mic B L/R → 3V3. If both are tied the same way, the two mics talk over each other on the data line.
  • All grounds are common: both mics, the amp and the ESP32.
  • Once flashed, run beep 1000 300 to test the speaker and doa 10 while clapping on each side to test the mics.
